BrainVault

Save everything. Find anything. Ask your own knowledge.

BrainVault is a private vault for everything worth keeping: notes, web pages, photos, PDFs, Word documents and voice notes. Search the text inside all of them, and ask a personal AI assistant that answers from what you've saved.

Save from anywhere

Your originals are kept in the vault, not just a summary, so you can open, share or save them back out whenever you need them.

📤

Share Sheet

Send links, text, photos and files from any app straight into a collection, with a note on why you saved it.

🖍️

Web Highlights

Select a passage in Safari and save just the quote, with a link back to the page.

📄

Documents

PDFs, Word (.docx), XML, Markdown, text and HTML. The text inside is read and made searchable.

📷

Photos and Scans

Take a photo or scan a document, receipt or whiteboard. Text in images is recognized on your device.

🎙️

Voice Notes

Speak a thought and BrainVault transcribes it into a searchable note.

💻

From Your Computer

Drop files into the BrainVault folder in iCloud Drive on your Mac or PC and they appear in your vault.

Find anything

Titles, notes, tags and the text inside PDFs, documents and photos, ranked by relevance. Search by voice, too.

Collections and Tags

Topics

People, companies, products and ideas found across your notes, gathered in one place.

Linked Notes

Spotlight, Siri and Widgets

Find notes from iPhone search, save with Siri and Shortcuts, and see recent notes on your Home Screen.

Resume Builder

Private by design

Yours alone

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your vault lives on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Sync across devices

Notes and files stay in sync on every iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to your Apple Account.

Face ID Lock

Require Face ID or Touch ID to open your vault.

Full Backups
