Saving to Your Vault

How do I save something from another app?

Tap the Share button in any app (Safari, Photos, Files, Mail and more) and choose BrainVault. Pick a collection, optionally add why you saved it, and tap Save. If BrainVault isn't in the list, scroll to the end of the app row, tap More and turn it on.

Which file types are supported?

PDF, Word (.docx), XML, plain text, Markdown, RTF, HTML, JSON (including exported AI chats) and images (JPEG, PNG, HEIC). BrainVault reads the text inside so you can search it. Older Word files (.doc) and password-protected documents can be stored but their text can't be read.

Is my original file kept, or only a summary?

The original is kept. Open the note and use the Original file card: Open to view it, or Share or Save to save it to Files, AirDrop it, or open it in another app.

How do I add files from my computer?

On your Mac or PC, put files in the BrainVault/Inbox folder in iCloud Drive. The next time you open BrainVault they're imported, and the originals are moved to Inbox/Imported so nothing is lost. You can check the status in Settings → From your computer.

The share sheet doesn't show my newest collection.

Open BrainVault once after creating or renaming a collection. The share sheet uses the list from your last visit.

Finding and Organizing

How do I search inside PDFs and photos?

Use the search field in the Library tab, or tap the microphone to search by voice. Search covers titles, notes, tags and the text found in documents and photos. Narrow results by tapping a collection or type.

How do I manage collections?

In Library, tap the sort button and choose Manage Collections, or go to Settings → Collections. You can create, rename, change icons or delete collections. Notes in a deleted collection move to Inbox.

How do I move or delete several notes at once?

In Library, tap Select, choose notes (or Select All), then use Move, Pin or Delete at the bottom of the screen.

AI Assistant

Which AI does BrainVault use?

BrainVault uses Apple Intelligence on your device, on supported iPhone, iPad and Mac models with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri). Optionally, you can connect your own AI server in Settings → AI Server.

The Assistant says "Not found in your vault."

The Assistant only answers from what you've saved, so it won't make things up. Try different words that appear in your note, or check the note's text in Library. For scanned PDFs and photos, give BrainVault a moment after saving to read the text.

A note's summary says it was written before my latest edit.

Tap Re-analyze on the summary to update it with your changes.

Sync, Storage and Backups

How do I sync between my iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on each device and keep iCloud Sync on in Settings. Files up to 25 MB sync to every device; larger files stay on the device they were saved on, and their text syncs.

How much space does BrainVault use?

See Settings → Storage. You can clear temporary copies there at any time; they're recreated when needed. iCloud space is shown in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

How do I back up or move my vault?

Settings → Export Vault creates a .zip with every note as Markdown (Obsidian-compatible), your original files, and a full backup file. To restore, choose Settings → Restore from Backup and pick the .zip.

I deleted a note by mistake.

Go to Settings → Recently Deleted and swipe right to restore it. Deleted notes are kept for 30 days.

BrainVault Pro

What does Pro unlock?

Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the 20-note limit of the free plan, on all your devices. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you bought Pro with. In BrainVault, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchase.

The purchase won't go through.

Check Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases → In-app Purchases is set to Allow. On a Family Sharing child account, the organizer may need to approve the purchase.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Can anyone else read my vault?

No. There's no BrainVault account or server. Your vault is stored on your devices and, with iCloud Sync, in your own private iCloud. See the Privacy Policy for details.
