Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2026 (cloud AI providers)

BrainVault is built to keep your knowledge private. There is no BrainVault account and no BrainVault server. The developer does not collect, see, sell or share your notes, files or any personal data. AI runs on your device with Apple Intelligence unless you choose to connect your own cloud AI provider.

1. What You Store in BrainVault

Everything you save (notes, links, web highlights, photos, scans, PDFs and other documents, voice note transcripts, Assistant chats, your About Me and Career Profile) is stored:

  • On your device, inside BrainVault's private storage.
  • In your own iCloud, if iCloud Sync is on (Settings → Sync). BrainVault uses your private iCloud database so your vault stays in sync on devices signed in to your Apple Account. This data is protected by Apple and is not accessible to the developer. Apple handles it under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can turn iCloud Sync off at any time.

2. Permissions

BrainVault asks for permission only when you use a feature that needs it:

  • Camera, to take photos and scan documents into your vault.
  • Photos, to import photos you pick. BrainVault only sees the photos you choose.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition, to record voice notes, search by voice and talk to the Assistant.
  • Face ID or Touch ID, if you turn on the vault lock. Biometric data never leaves Apple's secure hardware; BrainVault only learns whether unlocking succeeded.
  • Notifications, if you turn on review reminders or the weekly digest. These are scheduled on your device.

3. What Happens on Your Device

  • Reading text in photos and scanned documents (text recognition), and reading PDFs and Word documents.
  • Search, topics, related notes, duplicate detection and summaries made with Apple's on-device language tools.
  • The Assistant, when it uses Apple Intelligence: your questions and notes are processed on your device.
  • Spotlight search, widgets, Siri and Shortcuts integration, using the system's on-device indexes.

4. What Leaves Your Device

  • Web pages you save: when you save a link, BrainVault downloads that page from its website to read the title and text, just like opening it in a browser.
  • Speech recognition: voice is transcribed on your device when your device supports it. Otherwise, Apple's speech recognition service may process the audio, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Optional cloud AI provider: BrainVault uses on-device Apple Intelligence by default. If you add your own API key for a cloud AI provider in Settings → AI Provider (Claude by Anthropic, ChatGPT by OpenAI, Gemini by Google, or Grok by xAI), then whenever that provider is used, BrainVault sends it your question, the relevant excerpts of your notes (or the note you ask it to analyze or summarize), and your About Me and Career Profile details when a feature needs them. It is used only if you choose "Cloud AI provider" as the AI engine, or on a device that can't run Apple Intelligence. Requests go directly from your device to the provider over an encrypted connection, never through BrainVault, and are billed to your account with that provider. The provider handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy, including how long it keeps it and whether it may use it to improve its models, which can differ between free and paid plans: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI. Your API key is stored only in your device's Keychain and is never synced or sent anywhere except to that provider. If you don't add a key, nothing is sent.
  • Custom server (advanced): if you instead connect your own BrainVault-compatible server, the same information is sent to the server address you entered, which you choose and control.
  • Purchases: BrainVault Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; BrainVault only receives confirmation that the purchase is valid.
  • Sharing and exports: files or text leave BrainVault only when you share or export them yourself.

5. What BrainVault Does Not Do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No sharing or selling of data.
  • BrainVault does not use your content to train AI models.

6. Your Control and Deleting Data

  • Delete any note in the app. It moves to Recently Deleted for 30 days, and you can delete it permanently sooner.
  • Export your whole vault, including original files, at any time from Settings → Export Vault.
  • Deleting the app removes BrainVault's data from that device. To remove the copy in iCloud, go to iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → BrainVault.

7. Children

BrainVault does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
